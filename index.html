What we build

Three ways into the shop.

Every piece is drawn, milled and finished by the same two hands that pick up the phone.

Custom Furniture

Tables, cabinets and built-ins made to the inch for your space, not the showroom floor. We draw it, mill it, and deliver it ready to live with.

Restoration & Repair

Wobbly chairs, sun-bleached tabletops, drawers that stopped sliding a decade ago. We rebuild the joints and refinish the surface so the piece holds for another fifty years.

Wood & Material Sourcing

Reclaimed barnwood, air-dried walnut, hardwoods bought direct from small mills. We source what a project actually needs instead of whatever's on the shelf.

How a piece gets made

Three steps, start to delivery.

01

Walk the piece or space

We come to you, take measurements or look over the piece, and talk through what it needs to hold up.

02

Build in the shop

Milling, joinery and finishing happen on our bench, with photos along the way so you're not guessing.

03

Deliver and set up

We deliver, place it, and walk you through care — oil, wax, or whatever the wood actually needs.
